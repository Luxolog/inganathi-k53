Inganathi Driving Academy Privacy Notice

Version 1.0 · 16 November 2026

Before live collection: Confirm the legal entity operating the academy, Information Officer/privacy contact, Google account ownership and access list, retention period, and any cross-border processing. Replace this draft with the academy's approved notice.

1. Responsible party

Inganathi Driving Academy is the intended responsible party for information submitted through its learner survey and enquiry channels. Its legal entity name and verified privacy contact must be published before live collection. Ekhaya Technologies may provide technical services as an operator if it processes information on the academy's instructions.

2. Information collected

The learner survey may collect your name, cellphone number, optional email, area/town, age group, education or work-sector responses, licence journey, service interests, referral source and contact preferences. The K53 practice-test page additionally requests your name, surname, cellphone number and 13-digit South African ID number. An ID number is high-risk personal information and must only be collected where the academy has documented why it is necessary. Do not provide another person's contact details unless they have agreed to be contacted.

3. K53 test results

For the K53 practice test, the candidate details (including the ID number) are included in a result payload sent to the configured Google Apps Script result-email endpoint for test-result administration. The academy must verify endpoint ownership, access permissions, transport and storage security, recipient mailbox security, retention and deletion before live use. Do not use this feature for official licensing or government identity verification.

4. Purposes and lawful basis

Information is used to administer the survey, understand demand for learner and driver training, respond to requests for information and arrange follow-up where requested. The academy must document the applicable POPIA lawful basis for each purpose. The required survey acknowledgement confirms that you have read this notice; it is not blanket consent for unrelated uses.

5. Optional marketing

The marketing checkbox is optional and separate from the survey response. Leaving it unticked does not prevent survey submission. You may opt out of marketing at any time using the unsubscribe method in the message or by contacting the academy.

6. Storage, access and service providers

The survey prototype is configured to submit responses to a Google Apps Script endpoint intended to store them in a private Google Sheet. Before live use, verify the endpoint, sheet owner, sharing permissions, authorised users, Google processing terms and any cross-border transfer safeguards. Do not make the sheet publicly accessible.

7. Retention and security

Limit access to authorised academy personnel, use strong account security and MFA, and retain responses only for a documented period necessary for the stated purposes or legal obligations. Securely delete or de-identify information when no longer needed.

8. Your rights

Subject to POPIA, you may request access to or correction of your information, object to certain processing, withdraw consent where applicable, and request deletion where legally permitted. Contact the academy using its verified privacy contact, which must be added before launch. You may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

9. Children

If the survey is completed by a child, the academy must assess POPIA's requirements for children's personal information and obtain prior authorisation from a competent person where required. Do not collect children's information until this process is approved.

10. Notice updates

This notice may change as the survey, providers or purposes change. The academy must publish the current version and contact details wherever information is collected.
